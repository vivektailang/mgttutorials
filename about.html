About the tutor

A little about Megha

A biology mentor who blends academic depth with compassionate teaching.

Megha Goswamy is a biology educator with a strong foundation in teaching and research. Her academic journey began with a degree from Kasturba Medical College, Manipal, and she has continued to build expertise in assisting students with both conceptual understanding and exam performance.

She has worked with learners across school and college levels, especially in life sciences, where she focuses on helping students connect textbook learning to practical, real-world biological understanding. Her classes are structured to build confidence, improve retention, and develop clear scientific thinking.

With a combination of experience, subject knowledge, and a student-first approach, Megha is committed to making biology learning approachable, engaging, and outcome-focused for every learner.

The classroom approach

Clear steps, strong fundamentals, and calm encouragement.

Megha believes that effective biology learning happens when students feel confident enough to ask questions and revisit ideas without pressure. Her teaching style is structured, supportive, and rooted in clarity.

Each session focuses on simplifying difficult concepts, improving retention through active recall, and helping students build a deeper understanding of the subject rather than just memorizing facts.
