A little about Megha

Biology taught with clarity and care.

Megha Goswamy is an educator and an alumnus of Kasturba Medical College, Manipal. She brings more than 15 years of teaching experience with MBBS, MD and Allied Health Sciences students.

Her approach is rooted in making concepts understandable and connecting foundational Biology with medical applications. She encourages students to ask questions, build their understanding step by step and become more confident learners.

Classes are focused on Biology for students in Classes 11 and 12, with limited student numbers to support personalised attention.
